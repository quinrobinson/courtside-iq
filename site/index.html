    No team scores, no leaderboards.

    Youth sports has plenty of box scores and rankings. Courtside IQ is built around one player and their own growth, and that shapes what it refuses to show.

    • No team scores

      The app follows your player, not the scoreboard. There is no final score to log and nothing to win or lose.

    • No leaderboards

      Your player is never ranked against teammates or other kids. Growth IQ is a read on one player, not a place in line.

    • Measured for their age

      A 9-year-old and a 16-year-old are each read against their own age group, so the numbers mean the same thing at every age.

    • Private to your family

      Profiles belong to your account. No public pages, no shared rankings, nothing posted anywhere.

    Questions parents ask

    What ages is it for?

    Players from 8 to 18. Every rating is read against your player's own age group, so a 9-year-old and a 16-year-old are each measured fairly for where they are.

    What does the free plan include?

    One player and three games, each with its full read, so you can see exactly what Courtside IQ does with a game. Growth IQ needs five games to mean something, so it arrives with Premium, along with unlimited games and up to three players.

    Why can't I see a Growth IQ yet?

    Growth IQ needs five games before it shows, so one big or quiet night can't define your player. Until then, every game still gets its own insight. On the free plan, Premium unlocks the games that get you there.

    Is my player compared to other kids?

    No. There are no leaderboards and no team scores. Growth IQ reads your player against what is typical for their age and against their own recent games, never against teammates.

    Who can see my player's information?

    Player profiles belong to your account and there are no public profiles. Our privacy policy explains exactly what is stored and why.

    Do I need signal in the gym?

    No. Track the whole game without a connection and it syncs when you are back online. The insight arrives once it does.

    How do I cancel Premium?

    Cancel any time in your app store subscription settings. On the monthly plan, cancelling before the 7-day trial ends means you are not charged.
